Rules that make it this site and no other

Colour is wool.Every colour has a dye name in French and Arabic. The interface uses rose, indigo and madder; mint and turmeric appear as bands and threads, never behind a product photo.
The chain carries the page.A thick crochet chain separates sections and changes colour with them. Its only motion: it is drawn stitch by stitch once on first load. With reduced motion it is simply there.
One piece at a time.Each one-of-a-kind piece gets its own large row with its name and the yarn colours used, instead of a four-column grid of two products.
Board for review only. No photos here are real; the striped boxes mark where your mom's photos go.
